01 — About

Where this starts

My background is mechanical engineering — a Bachelor's and Master's, followed by several years in the space industry. I moved through quality management as an engineer, then product assurance, then into security accreditation, then security manager. I currently work as a Service Level Manager.

02 — Experience

The road here

Engineering and the space industry, moving steadily toward security.

  1. Service Level Manager

    Current

    Managing service delivery and performance against agreed commitments — the operational, cross-functional side of keeping complex systems accountable.

  2. Security Manager

    Owned security posture and decision-making for the area under my remit — the role where security stopped being adjacent and became the actual job.

  3. Security Accreditation

    Assessed systems against formal security requirements and drove them through accreditation — close, detailed work with risk, controls, and evidence.

  4. Product Assurance

    Quality and assurance work on space hardware and systems — rigor, traceability, and process discipline that carried directly into how I approach security now.

  5. Quality Management Engineer

    First role in the space industry, straight out of engineering school — where the habit of methodical, evidence-based work started.

  6. M.Sc., Mechanical Engineering

    Graduate degree building on the Bachelor's — deeper systems thinking, the last academic step before moving into the space industry.

  7. B.Sc., Mechanical Engineering

    The foundation underneath all of it — an engineering way of thinking about systems, failure modes, and how things actually break.
